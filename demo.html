About LittleSeed

Free Bible coloring sheets for children & missionaries worldwide

Our Mission

LittleSeed exists to put quality Bible teaching materials into the hands of every missionary, Sunday school teacher, and church worker — regardless of location, budget, or internet speed.

Every coloring sheet is completely free to download, print, and use without restriction. No subscriptions, no watermarks, no sign-up required.

How to Use

1
Search

Type a Bible story, theme, or keyword — Noah, Easter, forgiveness — and find matching sheets instantly.

2
Choose your language

Select from 9 languages. The verse text changes — no separate image needed.

3
Download & print

Click Download PDF for a print-ready file, or Print to send directly to your printer.
